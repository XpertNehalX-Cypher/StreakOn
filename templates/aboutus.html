We built this because consistency is the hardest thing to maintain alone — so we made it a shared experience.

What Is StreakOn

Not a suggestion.
A commitment.

Every day at midnight, everyone gets the same challenge. Whether it's 50 pushups, a cold shower, or writing three things you're grateful for — you either did it or you didn't. No excuses. No personal bests only you can see. Everyone, same challenge, same day.

We're not a fitness app.
We're not a productivity app.
We're a consistency app.

Small daily actions compound into real change — and doing them alongside thousands of others makes them stick. The App exists to close the gap between who you are and who you want to be, one day at a time.

Why We Built It

We got tired of
starting things.

Both of us had notebooks full of goals, apps full of todos, and a track record of quitting by week two. StreakOn was built out of frustration with ourselves — and a belief that if other people were doing the same thing as you, skipping would feel very different.
